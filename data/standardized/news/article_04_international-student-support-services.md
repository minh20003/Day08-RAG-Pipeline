## International student support services

Home / Study at RMIT / International students / International student support services

### RMIT Vietnam offers specialised support to all international students studying with us.

### Free airport pick-up service

RMIT offers you a free airport pick-up service upon your arrival in Vietnam.

### Complimentary activities

These include a welcome dinner, city tour, cultural discovery trips, cooking classes and ceramic/mosaic workshops

### Your own buddy

International students are assigned a buddy prior to their first semester. Buddies support international students for their entire stay in Vietnam.

### Orientation Week

Join Orientation Week for information on RMIT student services and networking activities.

### Student life and services

There are plenty of opportunities to create a rich, rewarding and fulfilling experience at RMIT Vietnam.

### Library services

The library houses over 700,000 offline and online resources and hosts several archives and digital exhibitions.

### Career and job support

RMIT has services that can help you to develop your skills, plan for your future career and get promising job opportunities.

### Sports and social groups

RMIT Vietnam boasts around 60 student-run clubs and societies, covering a range of sports, cultural, business and social interests.

### Student activities and events

We organise hundreds of events and activities each year, from large scale concerts and festivals to art exhibitions, talk shows and community trips.
